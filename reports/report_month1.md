During the first month of the project, we made a literature review on tokamak physics, with particular emphasis on MHD stability and plasma diagnostics. In John Wesson (2003), we studied the sections on:

MHD stability: to develop an understanding of the destabilizing forces, stability theory, and the energy principle.
Tokamak diagnostics: to understand the relevant physical information that can be obtained from the different diagnostics during a plasma discharge.

We also studied Waelbroeck (2009) to develop a better understanding of magnetic islands and their associated equilibrium structures, which are the main focus of this project. Fitzpatrick (2011, 1995) was also studied to understand the physical mechanisms responsible for the formation and evolution of magnetic islands, as well as their effects on plasma confinement.

In parallel with the theoretical study, we gained access to the TCABR MDSplus server and learned how to retrieve data from historical discharges. We investigated the diagnostics available in the TCABR database and their relevance to the objectives of the present work. Finally, we performed an initial analysis of Soft X-ray, $H_\alpha$, and Mirnov coil signals. In the latter, we were able to identify the main spectral characteristics associated with a candidate $m/n=2/1$ mode, including its characteristic frequency range and temporal evolution.
